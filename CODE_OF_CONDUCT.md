# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a reference document for people trying to figure out what is actually free and what they are allowed to do with it. Accuracy is the whole product. Be precise, and be reasonable.

## Expected behaviour

- **Cite sources.** "I think X is non-commercial" is a guess. "X's LICENSE.md says Y" is a contribution.
- **Label your confidence.** Say whether you verified something or believe it.
- **Disagree about facts, not people.** Licensing disputes are settled by reading the file.
- **Assume good faith.** People re-reporting a broken link are helping.

## Not acceptable

- Posting someone's private information, or a "free AI" tool whose main feature is generating likenesses of real people without consent.
- Recommending tools whose stated purpose is deception, impersonation or non-consensual synthetic media.
- Padding issues with link spam or affiliate content.
- Harassment of any kind.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests and discussions in this repository.

## Enforcement

Report via a private GitHub message to the maintainer. Reports are reviewed and acted on. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues or contributions that break this standard, and may ban contributors who continue.
